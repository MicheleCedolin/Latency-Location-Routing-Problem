## Mechanism (engine `llrp_fast` v7 = v6 + `poolsp.hpp`; everything behind flags, off by default)

- Flags: `--pool-sp`, `--pool-tl S` (3 s CPU per call), `--pool-sets M` (3), `--pool-first N` (8), `--pool-src 1|2`, `--pool-theta X` (0.02), `--pool-nodes N` (300000). The arm B1 is `--pool-sp --pool-tl 3 --pool-sets 3 --pool-first 8 --pool-src 1`, added to the flags of V1.
- Pool: the routes of the final solution of every GRASP iteration (key: depot + customer set); when a route is added, its order is polished by an exact dynamic programme (Held-Karp, k <= 16).
- Set partitioning (SP): at iteration 8, 16, 32, ... and at the end of the run; for the best M open-depot sets seen, a Lagrangian bound (<= 4000 subgradient steps), elimination by reduced cost against the
  upper bound, and an exact depth-first search (nodes <= 300000, time <= `--pool-tl`). A result is adopted only after `check_validity` and a strict improvement. SP uses no randomness.
- CPU fairness: the SP time is INSIDE the CPU limit (the time for the last call is reserved; intermediate calls are deducted from the same counter).

## Protocol

57 instances with n > 75, arm B1, seeds 1..30, CPU limit max(5, 3n) s including the SP time (the ext10 budget): 1710 tasks, about 203 CPU-hours. Reference: the 1710 runs of stage 3 (arm V1; not re-run).

## Pilot criteria (one instance, P121122-20; arms B1 and B2 (`--pool-src 2`, exploratory), 30 seeds x 600 s)

- P0: one-sided Mann-Whitney (B1 < V1) p < 0.01 and mean gap drop >= 0.15 points against V1.
- P1: at least one run within BKS + 0.005.
- P2: at least 5 of 30 runs <= Zou f_avg.
- S: 30/30 runs feasible and independently verified; CPU overrun: mean <= 1 %, maximum <= 2 %.
- Reading: if P0 passes, B1 is a candidate for a single-run component (the decision on the full campaign lies with the user); if it does not pass, only the post-hoc column "SP of 30" (a multi-start aggregation, labelled separately from the single-run result) is reported.
- Reference of the pilot (V1, job 153847, P121122-20, 30 seeds, 600 s): mean gap 0.792 %, best 0.110 %, 0/30 runs within BKS + 0.005, 2/30 runs <= Zou f_avg (5592.63).
- The pilot (60 runs) is not archived in this repository and its outcome is not restated here; the criteria of the full campaign below do not depend on it.

## Pre-registered criteria of the full campaign (B1 against V1)

| criterion | definition |
|---|---|
| **G1** hard class | the mean gap over the 30 instances with n >= 150 is lower by >= 0.10 points than that of V1 |
| **G0** overall improvement | one-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank on the instance-level gaps (B1 < V1) p < 0.01 **and** the mean over the 57 instances drops by >= 0.10 points |
| **Z** harm | the number of instances more than 0.2 points WORSE than V1 is <= 5 of 57 |

Not criteria (descriptive): the class table, hits (best of 30 <= BKS + 0.005), the comparison with Zou (hardware not normalised), the anytime values and the set-partitioning log (`pool.csv`: columns `complete` and `improved`).
Known risk written down in advance: `--pool-tl 3` CPU seconds on the cluster (about 8 times slower than the development machine) means about 8 times fewer depth-first nodes than in the development tests, so the late SP calls may be weaker; `pool.csv` records whether each call was completed.
